# Earth imagery

EOxCloudless 2016 provides an almost cloud-free, natural-colour mosaic assembled from Sentinel-2 observations taken in 2016 and 2017. The 10 m ground sampling distance supports regional and city-scale views. UnoPixelMap requests 256 × 256 Web Mercator JPEGs through zoom 14 and magnifies them for closer camera positions. Imagery dates and building-model dates differ. [Mosaic production](https://eox.at/2017/08/sentinel-2-global-cloudless-mosaic/)

The world overview, nine Lower Manhattan tiles and nine northern Lake Garda tiles are packaged for offline demonstrations. The Garda z12 cluster covers x=2170–2172 and y=1458–1460 around Riva del Garda. All nineteen JPEG payloads are unchanged from the public 2016 service. The map projects the imagery into the selected camera view. The remaining visible tiles stream from EOX on demand. Cached tiles are held in memory, with four concurrent fetches and a 4 MiB encoded-tile budget. [WMTS capabilities](https://tiles.maps.eox.at/wmts/1.0.0/WMTSCapabilities.xml)
